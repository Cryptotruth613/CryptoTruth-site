Did you hear it? Probably not. That is the point. A bank failure used to be front page news. Now it barely makes a regional business section, and when it does, the story is always the same: depositors are protected, the system is sound, nothing to see here.

## Not a scandal today

Each failure on its own looks manageable. Count them together and a pattern starts to show. The quiet is not a sign that the problem is small. It is a sign that someone would prefer you not add them up.

> Trust is the real scarcity. Every quiet failure spends a little more of it.

## 2008 repeat?

History does not repeat exactly, but the setup rhymes:

- Losses that stay off the books until they can't.
- Reassurance in place of explanation.
- A rescue that arrives before the public understands what is being rescued.

## What'll they do this time?

The tool on the shelf now is the central bank digital currency. Sold as safety and convenience. Built for visibility and control. Sound money asks a simpler question: who holds the keys?
