About This Site

No trackers. No cookies. No analytics. This site does not count you, profile you, or sell you. There are no hidden scripts from ad networks or social media companies, and no fonts pulled from anyone else's servers. The pages, the memes, and the articles all come from one place, and nothing about your visit is sent anywhere.

Phase one: ordinary hosting, for now

Right now this site runs on ordinary infrastructure: the files live on GitHub and are served by GitHub Pages. That was a deliberate trade. It gets the site up and publishing quickly while the harder work happens in the background.

It also means the site still has a single point of failure. Any company that hosts it could, in principle, throttle it, redirect it, or take it down. That is true of almost every website you visit. It should not be true of this one for long.

Phase two: nobody's permission required

The plan is to move the site onto decentralized storage (IPFS), with copies held by several independent services, so no single company can remove it. It will also get a second address on the Ethereum Name Service, one that no domain registrar controls. If cryptotruth.co is ever blocked or seized, the content will still be reachable.

Same principle as self-custody, applied to words instead of coins: don't trust a third party to keep what matters to you.

Why say any of this?

Because a site about first principles should be honest about its own. You deserve to know where it stands today and where it is going.
